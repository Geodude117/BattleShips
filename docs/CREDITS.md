# Credits

## Sound effects

All sound effects are by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and released under
[CC0 1.0 (public domain)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Credit isn't required, but it's appreciated.

| In the game | File | Kenney pack | Original file |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Firing a shot | `fire.m4a` | Sci-Fi Sounds | `thruster_fire_000` (trimmed to 0.9s) |

The files were converted from OGG/WAV to AAC (`.m4a`) so iOS can play them.
They were downloaded from these CC0 mirrors of Kenney's packs:
[Sci-Fi Sounds](https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-for-godot),
[Interface Sounds](https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds),
[Digital Audio](https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-digital-audio-for-godot).
